The Game stores gameplay progress and settings locally on your device. Depending on the Game version, this may include your score or record, gameplay progress, Diamond balance, unlocked or selected aircraft, aircraft upgrades, and game settings.

The Game does not provide cloud synchronization for gameplay progress. Local data may be lost if you uninstall the Game, clear its app data, change devices, or if the device or operating system removes the data. We do not promise that locally stored progress can be recovered.

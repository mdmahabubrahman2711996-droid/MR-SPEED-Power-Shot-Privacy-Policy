Privacy Policy

Game: MR SPEED: POWER SHOT

Developer: Mr Speed Studio

Effective Date: October 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how MR SPEED: POWER SHOT ("the Game"), developed by Mr Speed Studio ("we", "us", or "our"), handles information when you use the Game.

1. Information Stored by the Game

The Game does not require account registration. It may store game progress and settings locally on your device, including high scores, gameplay statistics, Red Diamond balance, unlocked or selected aircraft, and sound, music, and haptics preferences. This information is used to operate the Game and remember your progress.

2. Advertising and Google AdMob

The Game uses Google AdMob to display advertisements, including banner ads and optional rewarded video ads. Banner ads may appear on the Home Screen and Game Over Screen.

The Game also provides a "Watch Ads Get Diamond" option. If you choose to watch and successfully complete a rewarded advertisement, the Game is designed to award 5 in-game Red Diamonds. No reward is intended to be granted when an advertisement is cancelled, fails, or is not completed.

Google AdMob and its advertising partners may automatically collect or receive certain information when ads are requested, displayed, or interacted with. Depending on your device, settings, region, and the advertising configuration, this may include IP address, device or advertising identifiers, ad interaction information, diagnostic information, and related technical data.

This information may be used to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud and abuse, and provide personalized or non-personalized advertising as permitted by applicable settings and laws.

For more information, please review Google's Privacy Policy and Google's Advertising Policies and Technologies.

3. In-App Purchases and Google Play Billing

The Game offers optional purchases of Red Diamonds through Google Play Billing. Google Play handles payment processing. We do not receive or store your full payment card details.

The Game may store purchase-related records locally to help prevent duplicate rewards and maintain your in-game balance. Red Diamonds are virtual items for use within the Game only. They have no cash value and cannot be withdrawn or exchanged for real money.

4. How Information Is Used

Information described in this policy may be used to save game progress and settings, provide purchased virtual items, display and measure advertisements, help prevent fraud, and maintain the Game's operation.

5. Third-Party Services and Data Sharing

Google Play Billing and Google AdMob may process information necessary to provide their services. These services handle information according to their own privacy policies and terms. We do not sell your locally stored game progress.

6. Permissions

The Game uses internet access to support advertising and Google Play Billing. It may use vibration permission for haptic feedback. The Game's core gameplay does not require access to your contacts, camera, microphone, or precise location.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored game data generally remains on your device until you clear the Game's data, uninstall the Game, or the operating system removes it. Information processed by Google AdMob or Google Play is subject to Google's retention and deletion practices.

8. Children's Privacy

The Game does not intentionally ask children to submit personal information directly. Advertising services may process device or advertising information as described in this policy. Applicable age restrictions, parental consent requirements, and advertising rules must be followed for the user's region.

9. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect locally stored game data. However, no electronic storage or transmission method is completely secure. Third-party services follow their own security practices.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the Game or its services change. The latest version will be published at this Privacy Policy webpage. Please review it periodically.

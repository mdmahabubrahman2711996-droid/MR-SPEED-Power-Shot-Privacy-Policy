Privacy Policy

MR SPEED: POWER SHOT

Developer: MR SPEED STUDIO

Effective Date: October 9, 2026

MR SPEED STUDIO ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how MR SPEED: POWER SHOT handles information when you use our mobile game, including advertising services and in-app purchases.

1. Information We Collect

The game does not require you to create an account or provide your name, email address, phone number, home address, or password to play its core gameplay.

However, third-party services integrated into the game, including Google AdMob and Google Play Billing, may collect or process certain information as described in this policy.

Depending on the services, SDK versions, device settings, and configuration, information may include:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate a device's general location.
  • Advertising identifiers and other device or app identifiers.
  • Interactions with the game and advertisements, such as app launches, ad impressions, taps, and video views.
  • Diagnostic and performance information, such as app or SDK errors and performance data.
  • Purchase-related information needed to process and validate in-app purchases.

We do not intentionally ask users to provide precise location, contacts, photos, camera recordings, or microphone recordings for the game's core gameplay. Third-party SDK behavior and Android permissions may vary by implementation.

2. Game Data Stored on Your Device

The game may store gameplay progress and settings locally on your device, including:

  • Best score and gameplay progress.
  • Red Diamonds balance.
  • Unlocked and selected aircraft.
  • Sound, music, and haptic settings.
  • Enemy, elite enemy, and boss statistics.
  • Gameplay or survival time.
  • Purchase-processing records used to help prevent duplicate rewards and recover eligible purchases.

This information is used to provide game features, preserve settings and progress, and process purchases. Locally stored records may be lost if you clear the app's data or uninstall the game.

3. Google AdMob and Advertising

MR SPEED: POWER SHOT uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. These may include banner advertisements and optional rewarded video advertisements.

The game may offer a "Watch Ads Get Diamond" feature. If you successfully complete an eligible rewarded advertisement, the game is designed to award 5 Red Diamonds. Rewards are subject to successful ad completion and the game's reward-processing rules. No reward is intended for an advertisement that is cancelled, fails, or is not completed.

Google's Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and process certain information to provide advertisements, measure ad performance, diagnose technical issues, and help detect fraud. Depending on the SDK version and configuration, this may include:

  • IP address and approximate general location inferred from it.
  • Advertising ID, app set ID, or other supported device or app identifiers.
  • Ad impressions, video views, taps, and other product interactions.
  • Diagnostic information and SDK performance data.

Google and its advertising partners may use information as permitted by applicable settings, consent choices, and their own privacy policies to provide, measure, and protect advertising services. Whether advertisements are personalized depends on app configuration, applicable consent requirements, and user settings.

For more information about Google's data practices, please visit:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services

4. In-App Purchases and Google Play Billing

The game may offer virtual Red Diamond packages through Google Play Billing. Payments are processed by Google Play and are subject to Google's applicable terms and policies.

We do not directly receive or store your full payment card number, bank account details, or payment credentials through the game.

The game may process purchase information, including purchase tokens and purchase status, to identify eligible purchases, grant the correct virtual items, prevent duplicate rewards, and recover eligible purchases where supported.

Red Diamonds are virtual in-game items. They have no cash value, cannot be withdrawn, and cannot be exchanged for real money.

Google Play may process payment and purchase-related information under its own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

5. How Information Is Used

Information processed by the game and its integrated services may be used for the following purposes:

  • To provide gameplay and save game settings and progress.
  • To display and deliver advertisements.
  • To provide and measure rewarded advertisements.
  • To process purchases and deliver virtual items.
  • To prevent fraudulent activity and duplicate purchase rewards.
  • To diagnose errors and improve service reliability and performance.
  • To comply with applicable legal and platform requirements.

MR SPEED STUDIO does not sell users' personal information for money. We do not independently operate a user-account database for the game's core gameplay.

Information may be processed or shared by third-party providers as necessary for the services they provide, including:

  • Google Play Billing: To process purchases, confirm purchase status, and support purchase-related functionality.

These providers handle information according to their own policies, applicable settings, and legal obligations. Their processing practices are not controlled entirely by MR SPEED STUDIO.

Information handled by Google AdMob and Google Play is subject to the applicable provider policies and retention practices. For requests relating to information handled directly by Google, please consult Google's privacy resources.

Privacy Policy

MR SPEED: POWER SHOT

Developer: MR SPEED STUDIO

Effective Date: October 8, 2026

MR SPEED STUDIO ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how MR SPEED: POWER SHOT handles information when you use the game.

1. Personal Information

MR SPEED: POWER SHOT does not directly collect or require personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, home address, contacts, photos, videos, camera data, microphone data, location data, or account passwords.

2. Game Data Stored on Your Device

The game stores certain gameplay and settings information locally on your device to provide game functionality. This may include:

  • Best score
  • Red Diamonds balance
  • Unlocked aircraft
  • Selected aircraft
  • Sound settings
  • Music settings
  • Haptic/vibration settings
  • Total enemies defeated
  • Total elite enemies defeated
  • Total bosses defeated
  • Total gameplay/survival time
  • Purchase-processing records used to prevent duplicate rewards

This information is used to save your game progress, maintain your settings, provide gameplay features, and prevent duplicate rewards from purchases.

3. In-App Purchases

MR SPEED: POWER SHOT may offer virtual Red Diamond packages through Google Play Billing.

Payments are processed by Google Play. We do not directly receive or store your payment card number, bank account details, or other payment credentials.

The game may process Google Play purchase information, including a purchase token, to identify completed purchases, prevent duplicate rewards, recover eligible purchases, and grant the appropriate number of virtual Red Diamonds.

Red Diamonds are virtual in-game items. They have no cash value and cannot be exchanged for real money.

4. Data Sharing

MR SPEED STUDIO does not sell or rent your personal information to advertisers or data brokers.

The game itself does not directly collect personal information for advertising or marketing purposes.

Google Play may process purchase-related information according to Google's applicable privacy policies and terms.

5. Internet and App Permissions

The game may use internet access for Google Play Billing and related purchase functionality.

The app may use vibration or haptic functionality for gameplay feedback.

The app does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, location, or photos/videos.

6. Third-Party Services

The primary third-party service used by the game for purchases is Google Play Billing.

Google Play processes payment and purchase-related information according to Google's applicable policies and terms.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect locally stored gameplay and purchase-processing information. However, no electronic storage or transmission system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

The game's gameplay and settings information is stored locally on your device while it is needed for the game's functionality.

You can remove locally stored game data by using Android's app-data or storage controls or by uninstalling the game, subject to Android and Google Play behavior.

9. Children's Privacy

MR SPEED: POWER SHOT does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

The game does not require users to provide personal information such as their name, email address, phone number, address, or location in order to play.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when necessary. Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
